## Conversion toolchain image

The Docker image built from `conversion-toolchain/` (published as
`oaax-hailo-toolchain.tar`) contains Ubuntu 22.04 packages and the Python
packages installed from `conversion-toolchain/requirements.txt` (`lgg`, `onnx`,
`onnxsim` and their dependencies). They are not vendored in this repository and
keep their own licenses, which are included in the image. The proprietary Hailo
Dataflow Compiler and the HailoRT wheels/deb are not part of the image: you
mount them at run time under their own Hailo license.
